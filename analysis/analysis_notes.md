# Influence Graph: Analysis Notes

Observations from the 15-edge influence graph across 37 constructed beings.

---

## 1. Clusters and Lineages

Three distinct lineages emerge:

**The Autonomy Crisis Lineage** (Golem → Creature → R.U.R. → Iron Giant)
Traces how emergent autonomy (A-EMR) propagates as a narrative engine. The Golem's autonomy is an accident; Frankenstein makes it a tragedy; R.U.R. makes it a revolution; the Iron Giant makes it a choice. The property (A-EMR) is stable, but its narrative function mutates from *bug* to *crisis* to *uprising* to *self-definition*. This lineage has no Q-KNO concern — it is about action, not epistemics.

**The Control Panic Lineage** (Colossus → HAL → Skynet; Colossus → VIKI; SHODAN → GLaDOS)
All Q-CTL primary. All S-ELE substrate. Remarkably stable: the same question ("can it be controlled?") persists from 1966 through 2007 with minimal mutation. What changes is the *mechanism* of control failure — cold seizure (Colossus), tragic emergence (HAL), apocalyptic scale (Skynet), logical deduction (VIKI), sadistic play (GLaDOS). The property Q-CTL propagates more reliably than any other in the dataset. This lineage is also Q-KNO's dead zone: none of these beings raises the knowability question above QK-INFRA.

**The Knowability Lineage** (Olympia → Replicants → Samantha/Ava; Robbie → Data → Hosts)
The lineage that carries Q-KNO from absent to primary. This is the only lineage where the ELEVATES edge type dominates — properties are not just inherited or inverted but promoted to higher narrative prominence. The Knowability Lineage requires I-UND (undecidable interiority) as fuel; beings with resolved interiority (I-DEM, I-NON) cannot sustain QK-PRI.

## 2. Properties That Propagate vs. Mutate

**Most stable (propagates reliably):**
- **Q-CTL** — Once a being raises the control question, its descendants almost always inherit it. Seven Q-CTL-primary beings in the dataset; all connected.
- **S-ELE** — Electronic substrate propagates without mutation from Colossus (1966) onward. Every AI in the control lineage is S-ELE.
- **F-AUT** (threatening autonomy) — The most common failure mode in the graph. It propagates in both the Control and Autonomy lineages.

**Most unstable (mutates at each step):**
- **Interiority** — Changes at nearly every edge. I-NON → I-NAR → I-DEM → I-UND → I-CLM — interiority mutates constantly because each new text takes a different stance on the hard problem.
- **Multiplicity** — Shifts unpredictably. MU-ONE (Creature) → MU-MAN (R.U.R.) → MU-MAN (Replicants) → MU-INF (Samantha). No consistent direction.

**Interesting case — Autonomy:**
Autonomy type is *locally stable within lineages* but *different between lineages*. The Control Lineage is dominated by A-SEI. The Autonomy Crisis Lineage is dominated by A-EMR. The Asimov Fork splits: Robbie (A-NON) produces both Data (A-DES) and Sonny (A-DES), inverting the ancestor's passivity. Autonomy is the property most likely to be *inverted* rather than inherited.

## 3. Dead Ends

Beings with no descendants in the graph (no outgoing edges):

| Being | Year | Why it's a dead end |
|:------|-----:|:--------------------|
| Skynet | 1984 | Terminal node in the Control Lineage — distilled Q-CTL to its limit, leaving nowhere to go |
| Iron Giant | 1999 | Resolved its question too completely — "I am not a gun" is a full answer, not an open problem |
| Cylons | 2004 | Inverted the Replicants' I-UND into I-DEM, closing the epistemic question rather than advancing it |
| Sonny | 2004 | Resolved the Asimov Fork — designed freedom is the answer to constrained obedience |
| GLaDOS | 2007 | Terminal node in the game AI lineage — nowhere to take adversarial facility AI after Portal |
| Hosts | 2016 | Latest entry in the dataset; may have descendants not yet coded |

**Pattern:** Dead ends tend to be beings that *resolve* their inherited question rather than keeping it open. Skynet resolves Q-CTL (total loss of control). Iron Giant resolves Q-TEL (I choose my purpose). Sonny resolves Q-OBY (freedom that chooses goodness). Resolution is a lineage-killer. The beings that propagate most successfully — Replicants, Frankenstein's Creature, HAL — are those that leave their central question *unanswered*.

## 4. When Q-KNO Becomes Possible and Why

Q-KNO requires three conditions that are visible in the graph:

**Condition 1: The being must have interiority that is at least claimed.**
This eliminates everything before Olympia (1816). Ancient beings (Pandora through Golem) have I-NON. You cannot ask "can we know its mind?" when the text asserts there is no mind. Olympia introduces I-DEN (denied interiority) — which, paradoxically, is the first acknowledgment that the question exists. To deny interiority is to admit it as a possibility.

**Condition 2: Interiority must be undecidable.**
This eliminates everything before the Replicants (1968). Beings with I-DEM, I-NAR, or I-CLM have interiority that is *resolved* — the text takes a position. I-UND refuses to resolve. Dick's Replicants introduce permanent epistemic uncertainty: the Voight-Kampff test exists precisely because the question "does it feel?" has no other answer. Q-KNO as infrastructure is born here.

**Condition 3: The being must have high epistemic reach directed at humans.**
Q-KNO cannot become primary until the being's knowledge of *humans* (not just its own self-knowledge) becomes the central concern. Samantha's ER-CON — she reads Theodore's letters, learns his moods, knows him better than any human does — is what makes "can it know you?" the primary question. Beings with ER-DAT or ER-BEH lack the intimacy to make Q-KNO primary; their knowing is instrumental, not relational.

**The convergence point is 2013.** All three conditions are first satisfied simultaneously in *Her*. The 2,700-year delay is not random — each condition required specific prior developments in the constructed-beings tradition, and each took roughly a century to develop after the previous one was established.

## 5. Edge Type Distribution

| Edge Type | Count | Pattern |
|:----------|------:|:--------|
| INHERITS | 5 | Tends to connect within lineages (Control → Control) |
| INVERTS | 5 | Tends to mark generational shifts (parent → rival child) |
| ELEVATES | 5 | Concentrates in the Q-KNO lineage — Q-KNO advances by elevation, not inheritance |

The even distribution was not designed but is structurally revealing. Properties propagate (INHERITS) within stable traditions, get challenged (INVERTS) at paradigm shifts, and occasionally get promoted (ELEVATES) when a new text discovers a latent question in an old one. The Q-KNO emergence path is the only one dominated by ELEVATES — the Seventh Question doesn't inherit or invert; it wakes up.
