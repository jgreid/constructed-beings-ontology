Edge types

adaptsDirect adaptation of a specific source text (novel to film, etc.). The card values may shift but the being is the "same" being on a different medium.
sequelSame being, or a clearly derived successor being, in a later work in the same in-universe lineage. May coincide with property shifts (autonomy, divergence, primary_question).
inheritsLater work carries forward a named property cluster from the earlier work without being a direct adaptation or sequel. The lineage may be documented (author citations, interviews) or inferable from analytical fit.
invertsLater work shares the earlier work's lineage but flips one or more card axes — motivation reversed, primary question reinterpreted, a tonal inversion.
